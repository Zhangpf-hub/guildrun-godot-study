# Battle camera projection

The original Battle scene uses a physical camera. In level3, the real Camera is PathID 9518 and has Horizontal Gate Fit, a 21.946 × 16.002 mm film gate, a focal length of 43.169533 mm, and zero lens shift. All nine Cinemachine virtual cameras inherit the real camera's physical mode and agree on the horizontal gate, film dimensions, and lens shift. The nominal vertical angle is approximately 21°. The extractor records real-camera and virtual-camera properties separately in the generated battle camera data.

In the original native build, CinemachineBrain.PushStateToUnityCamera (0x4C5E480) sets physical mode at +0x48C, sensor dimensions at +0x4B2, gate fit at +0x4C0, and focal length at +0x4DF. CameraEffectiveRectApplier.Apply (0x6D4E20) caps the effective scene viewport at 7:3 with centered side bars. BattleCameraRatioPositionAdjuster.AdjustPositioning calls the same effective-aspect function (0x6D4470), so pose interpolation and lens fitting both use the capped aspect. Static board previews do not create the live battle camera controller.

Godot uses a vertical field of view. Horizontal Gate Fit instead preserves the angle subtended by the film width. Given the current film aspect \(s_w/s_h\) and the effective scene viewport aspect \(a\), multiply the tangent of the nominal vertical half-angle by \((s_w/s_h)/a\), then convert back to a full angle. Apply the mapping after interpolating the nominal angle so a viewport resize cannot bake the previous aspect into a running transition. [Unity Gate Fit documentation](https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.0/manual/cameras/physical/gate-fit/configure)

The projection tests use an independent pinhole reference. Transform the landmark \((4,3,-8)\) into each native camera's local frame by the inverse of its source pose. With \(W\) the width of the centered effective scene viewport, focal distance in pixels is \(W\times43.169533/21.945999\). For camera-local \((x,y,z)\) with forward along \(-z\), project to \((w/2+f x/(-z), h/2-f y/(-z))\) using the full-window center. The tests cover placement, fight, result and crossroads at 1024 × 768, 1280 × 720, and 1728 × 720. They also exercise resize partway through a blend, mouse selection of a projected gate after resize, and static preview.

Two real Steam Demo screenshots provided a separate 1022 × 769 comparison. The comparison used the original client area and measured matching gradients on static geometry. The stock Godot view was 2.99% and 2.79% larger in placement and combat. The source-derived correction left 0.17% and 0.49% scale differences. Residual translation varies between the two original camera states. The screenshots therefore provide no basis for adding fixed positional offsets or claim a perfect match. Original 16:9 HUD and camera framing still await direct capture. The source-based 16:9 projection and the Godot UI and gate interaction are covered by separate regression checks.
